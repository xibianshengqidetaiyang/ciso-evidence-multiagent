# Asset Classification Policy Demo

## Asset Inventory

The organization maintains an information asset inventory. The inventory should include asset name, asset owner, department, asset type, confidentiality level, integrity requirement, availability requirement, and review date.

## Classification Rules

Information assets are classified based on business impact and protection requirements. Typical classification factors include:

- confidentiality;
- integrity;
- availability;
- business criticality;
- personal information involvement;
- external sharing requirements.

## Review and Maintenance

Asset owners are responsible for reviewing asset records and classification results periodically. Changes to important assets should be approved and recorded.

## Example Evidence

- asset inventory;
- asset classification matrix;
- asset owner approval record;
- periodic review record.
