# Employee Handbook Demo

## Information Security Commitment

All employees must follow the organization's information security policies, procedures, and acceptable use requirements.

During onboarding, employees are required to acknowledge the employee handbook and sign an information security commitment form.

## Security Awareness Training

The organization provides annual information security awareness training to employees. Training topics include:

- acceptable use of information assets;
- phishing awareness;
- password and account protection;
- data protection requirements;
- incident reporting channels.

Training attendance and completion records should be retained by the HR or information security team.

## Review Evidence

Typical supporting evidence may include:

- employee handbook acknowledgment records;
- signed information security commitment forms;
- training plan;
- training attendance sheet;
- training test or questionnaire result.
